# Marketing & Distribution

## Core principle
Do not build the product first and then ask how to distribute it.

Distribution and product design should reinforce each other.

## Initial positioning
Avoid broad messaging such as:

> AI life-admin assistant

Prefer a specific outcome:

> Stop losing money without realizing it.

## Primary acquisition funnel
Best initial funnel:

Content/search/referral -> free instant tool -> useful result -> account -> watchlist -> permission -> monitoring -> subscription

Avoid:

Ad -> App Store -> Install -> Signup -> Setup -> Permissions -> Maybe value

## Free instant-use entry product
Landing page concept:

> Upload a bill, renewal email, contract, receipt, or subscription notice.

Watchtower returns:
- Price increase
- Deadline
- Renewal terms
- Potential cost
- Recommended action

No registration required for first result when practical.

## Channel 1 — Short-form educational content
Content should center on real pain discoveries.

Examples:
- “This harmless email could cost you $480/year.”
- “Three emails to search your inbox for today.”
- “Cancelled a subscription? Check this one thing.”
- “Your refund may have been approved but never returned.”

Preferred channels:
- TikTok
- Instagram Reels
- YouTube Shorts
- X
- Reddit where appropriate

CTA:

> Check yours free.

## Channel 2 — Free vertical tools / SEO
Create narrow public entry tools such as:
- Contract renewal analyzer
- Cancellation deadline checker
- Subscription increase detector
- Refund eligibility checker
- Warranty checker
- Return-window checker
- Airline compensation checker
- Lease notice-period checker
- Hidden-fee detector

Each tool should feed the same Watchtower account/watchlist system.

## Channel 3 — Shareable outcomes
Make results easy to share without exposing sensitive information.

Examples:
- “Watchtower found $1,482/year I was unnecessarily spending.”
- “Watchtower caught 6 costly issues this month.”

## Channel 4 — Referrals
Possible rewards:
- Free month
- Household trial
- Pro features temporarily unlocked

Prefer product-native referrals over generic discounts.

## Channel 5 — Partnerships
Potential partners:
- Personal-finance creators
- Newsletters
- Comparison sites
- Banks/fintechs
- Employee-benefits providers
- Accountants
- Mortgage brokers
- Travel platforms
- Consumer-rights organizations
- Credit monitoring services

## B2B2C opportunity
Possible employer benefit:

> Watchtower Premium included as an employee benefit.

One enterprise distribution relationship could create thousands of consumer users.

## Paid acquisition
Do not rely on paid ads before understanding:
- Visitor -> activation
- Activation -> account
- Account -> subscription
- Subscription retention
- CAC
- LTV

Use paid acquisition after the product has demonstrated repeatable activation and retention.

## Pre-development validation
Test multiple landing-page messages before deep engineering.

Example variants:
A. Stop wasting money.
B. Forward us your bills. We’ll find what you’re overpaying.
C. AI that watches your subscriptions, bills, and contracts.
D. Upload any contract. We’ll tell you what could cost you money.

Measure:
- Click-through
- Upload intent
- Email signup
- Completion rate
- Qualitative feedback

## Key growth hypothesis
Every pain can become an acquisition door:
- Refund
- Subscription
- Insurance
- Contract
- Warranty
- Renewal
- Travel

The user enters through one pain and remains because Watchtower protects them across many categories.
